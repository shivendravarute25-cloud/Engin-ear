RPM sweep analysis SIMULATION

A frequency component that changes with RPM may be engine-speed related. A component that stays nearly fixed may indicate resonance or another source.

Multi-RPM test OPTIONAL

Frequencies that move with RPM are engine or exhaust related. Frequencies that stay put while RPM changes are fixed-frequency components (resonance, electrical or mechanical). Give four RPM points, with or without a recording for each (empty slots use the lab simulation), or split the current signal as an RPM sweep.

About project

ENGINE EAR — VEHICLE SIGNAL INTELLIGENCE LAB. A Signals & Systems based educational platform for analyzing vehicle sound and vibration patterns using time-domain and frequency-domain techniques.

Concepts used: sampling, time-domain signals, Fourier transform and FFT, windowing, filtering, convolution, signal features, spectrogram, system response and stability.

Exhaust-aware logic: the analysis separates what the engine and exhaust harmonics explain from what they do not, so a loud or high-pitched performance exhaust is not read as a mechanical fault. It remains a screening tool.

Practical value: shows how signal processing can help notice unusual vehicle sound patterns and guide further inspection.
